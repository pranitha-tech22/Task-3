Who we are

People. Planet. Purpose.

Green Earth Foundation is a fictional, student-created environmental initiative designed to demonstrate how digital platforms can support community action. Our concept focuses on practical ways to make sustainability part of everyday life.

  • Make environmental learning accessible
  • Encourage local, community-led action
  • Promote responsible waste and resource use
